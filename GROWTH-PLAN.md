# Growth plan — Szkolenia Glocksklep

Based on a site review only (not traffic or sales data). Goal: turn visitors into paid bookings, then bring them back, then reach new people.

**Top three (do first):** online payment at signup → better course pages → follow-up emails.

---

## A. Turn visitors into bookings

1. **Online payment at signup** — Require payment when booking (BLIK + Przelewy24). A paid booking holds the seat and reduces no-shows. Show a clear cancellation policy next to pay.
2. **Cancellation policy** — Publish short, visible rules (e.g. full refund until X days before, credit after that, no refund for no-show). Link them from checkout and confirmation.
3. **Course pages that sell** — For each course, show: who it’s for, requirements (own gun, holster, round count), what you’ll be able to do afterwards, and 2–3 photos or a short clip from a real session.
4. **Social proof** — Short reviews from people who took the course, plus named outcomes where possible (e.g. “awansował do klasy A”).
5. **Visible scarcity** — Keep “wolne: N”. When seats are low, add “ostatnie miejsca”. For courses with no dates yet, show waitlist demand (how many are waiting).

## B. Bring people back

6. **Personal “Ścieżka rozwoju”** — After each course, mark what the user completed and suggest the next step so the path is a reason to book again.
7. **Passes and bundles** — Sell blocks (e.g. 3 individual sessions) or path packages at a discount.
8. **Gift vouchers** — Easy sale before Christmas and birthdays; redeemable for courses or 1:1 sessions.
9. **Automatic emails**
   - Reminder 2 days before the session
   - Post-course “jak poszło” with homework drills
   - Nudge when a new date opens for the next level

## C. Reach new people

10. **Short drill videos** — Instagram and YouTube clips that each link to the matching course.
11. **Free or cheap intro** — 1-hour assessment or 1:1 trial that ends with a clear “what to work on” — an easy first yes.
12. **Club and team deals** — Put the dedicated-groups offer in front of local IPSC clubs.
13. **Season-aware offer** — Autumn/winter: indoor dry-fire online sessions and winter 1:1s, not only waitlists when outdoor weather is a problem.

---

## Suggested order of work

| Phase | Items | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| 1 | 1, 2 | Paid seats = conversion + fewer no-shows |
| 2 | 3, 4, 5 | Course page converts traffic you already have |
| 3 | 9 | Cheap retention; supports path and next-date sales |
| 4 | 6, 7, 8 | Repeat purchase and gifts |
| 5 | 10–13 | Acquisition once the funnel and retention exist |

---

## Notes for implementation later

- Payment and emails likely need backend/API work (checkout, webhooks, templates), not only UI.
- Course-page content and scarcity can ship partly as CMS/copy before full product features.
- Path, passes, vouchers, and season offers are new catalog/commerce concepts on top of current enroll flow.
