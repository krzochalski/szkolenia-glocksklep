# Growth plan — Szkolenia Glocksklep

Based on a site review only (not traffic or sales data). Goal: turn visitors into paid bookings, then bring them back, then reach new people.

**Top three (do first):** online payment at signup → better course pages → follow-up emails.

---

## A. Turn visitors into bookings

1. **Online payment at signup** — Require payment when booking (BLIK + Przelewy24). A paid booking holds the seat and reduces no-shows. Show a clear cancellation policy next to pay.
2. **Cancellation policy** — Publish short, visible rules (e.g. full refund until X days before, credit after that, no refund for no-show). Link them from checkout and confirmation.
3. ~~**Course pages that sell** — For each course, show: who it’s for, requirements (own gun, holster, round count), what you’ll be able to do afterwards, and 2–3 photos or a short clip from a real session.~~
4. **Social proof** — Short reviews from people who took the course, plus named outcomes where possible (e.g. “awansował do klasy A”).
5. ~~**Visible scarcity** — Keep “wolne: N”. When seats are low, add “ostatnie miejsca”. For courses with no dates yet, show waitlist demand (how many are waiting).~~

## B. Bring people back

6. ~~**Personal “Ścieżka rozwoju”** — After each course, mark what the user completed and suggest the next step so the path is a reason to book again.~~
7. ~~**Post-course debriefing** — Class plan + extra drills in the participant panel (markdown WYSIWYG with images and YouTube). Only users marked as having finished that course can open it. One of the main features called out on the homepage (retention / “what you get after the course”).~~
8. ~~**Course-skill training** — Bookable training based on a course’s content: practice all the skills that were the subject of a course the person already took (refreshers / drill sessions, not a full re-run of the class).~~
9. **Passes and bundles** — Sell blocks (e.g. 3 individual sessions) or path packages at a discount.
10. **Gift vouchers** — Easy sale before Christmas and birthdays; redeemable for courses or 1:1 sessions.
11. **Automatic emails** Reminder 2 days before the session. Nudge when a new date opens for the next level.

## C. Reach new people

12. **Free or cheap intro** — 1-hour assessment or 1:1 trial that ends with a clear “what to work on” — an easy first yes.
13. **Club and team deals** — Put the dedicated-groups offer in front of local IPSC clubs.
14. **Season-aware offer** — Autumn/winter: indoor dry-fire online sessions and winter 1:1s, not only waitlists when outdoor weather is a problem. "Idzie zima" is the motto. Users can enroll for dryfire sessions. Need to create plans for manual training as well as some stage situational awareness exercises.
